Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

This policy covers the Box Score Daily website and the Box Score Daily Uploader, the tool that publishes videos to the Box Score Daily YouTube channel.

Who uses the Uploader

The Uploader is used only by the owner of the Box Score Daily channel, to upload videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not sign in or collect information from anyone else.

Use of YouTube API Services

The Uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What information we access and why

  • When the channel owner signs in with Google, the Uploader receives an access token with a single permission: to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope).
  • The Uploader uses that permission only to upload the videos we create, with their titles, descriptions and tags. It does not read, change or delete other videos, and it does not access comments, subscribers, analytics or account details.
  • The Uploader does not collect, request or store any information about YouTube viewers or other users.

How information is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer and the private server that runs the daily job, and is kept out of public code. Videos and their descriptions are stored in a private storage bucket. We do not sell, rent or share any of this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and does not collect personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account third-party connections page (also reachable through security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Revoking access makes the stored token unusable. To ask us to delete any stored data, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
