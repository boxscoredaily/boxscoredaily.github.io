Box Score Daily

Short recap videos of every NFL game, built from the box score and ready every morning.

What we make

  • Every game, ranked: after a full Sunday slate, a countdown from the least to the most exciting game.
  • Full recaps: the score, the quarters, who led when, the key stats, and the play that decided it.
  • Our own graphics only: stats and original designs, with no game footage, team logos or league marks.

Box Score Daily Uploader

Box Score Daily Uploader is the small tool we use to publish these videos. Each morning it creates the day's recap videos and uploads them to the Box Score Daily YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner to post to our own channel. It does not sign in other users and does not collect any viewer data.
